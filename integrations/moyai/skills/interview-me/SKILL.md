# Clarifying intent through an interview

Extract who is affected, which decision the user is trying to make, the outcome they care about and what they have already ruled out. Separate the stated request from your assumptions about the underlying need.

Ask one high-information question at a time. Explain the concrete trade-off or offer a plausible interpretation the user can correct. After each answer, refine the hypothesis and stop asking about points that are already settled. A useful final question distinguishes two genuinely different outcomes.

Summarize the intent in the user's words with success criteria, constraints and unresolved choices. In an interview task, invite correction of that statement before moving into design or implementation.
